Privacy Policy

Number Drop · Dungeons 80 · Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Data Controller & Contact

Number Drop and Dungeons 80 are developed and published by studio ailiers.

  • Controller: studio ailiers
  • Contact: contact@studioailiers.com

2. Offline & TestFlight Builds

In offline or TestFlight builds without advertising or analytics SDKs enabled, our games do not collect, transmit, or store personal data off your device. Game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. For beta builds distributed via Apple TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and tester feedback in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Live & Commercial Builds (AdMob, Firebase & StoreKit 2)

In live App Store releases that include advertising, analytics, remote configuration, and in-app purchases, our games may integrate the following services:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Serves advertisements. Depending on your consent and iOS tracking permissions, Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the iOS Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA or app-set ID), coarse IP-based location, ad interaction data, and diagnostic signals for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Remote Configuration): Measures aggregate gameplay progression, balance, app stability, and remote configuration parameters using app-instance identifiers, basic device information, and in-game event telemetry.
  • Apple StoreKit 2 (In-App Purchases): All payment transactions are processed directly by Apple through the App Store. studio ailiers never receives or stores your payment details.

To learn more about how Google processes data, please see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. GDPR / UK Consent (Google UMP) & iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT / IDFA)

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR / Google UMP): Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland are presented with a consent form via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are served. You can review, change, or withdraw your consent choices at any time from the Privacy Settings option in the game's settings menu.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT / IDFA): On iOS, the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is never accessed unless you grant permission in Apple's system prompt. You can revoke or change this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Legal Bases & Data Retention

Where GDPR / UK GDPR applies, data is processed based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising and tracking, legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for security, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics, and aggregated balance telemetry, and performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for in-app purchases and player support.

Local save data remains on your device until you reset progress or uninstall the app. Analytics event data in Google Firebase is retained for up to 14 months. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to withdraw consent at any time:

  • Update ad consent in the game's Privacy Settings or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data and app-scoped identifiers.
  • Contact us at contact@studioailiers.com.
