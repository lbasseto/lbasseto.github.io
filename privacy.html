2. Offline & TestFlight Builds

Dungeons 80 does not collect, transmit, or store personal data off your device. Game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. Number Drop's TestFlight and other test builds carry the same services as its live builds (described below), with Google's test ads, and send the same measures after consent, marked as coming from testers. For beta builds distributed via Apple TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and tester feedback in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. For test builds distributed through Google Play's testing tracks, Google may collect crash and performance data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Live & Commercial Builds (AdMob, Firebase & In-App Purchases)

In live App Store and Google Play releases that include advertising, analytics, remote configuration, and in-app purchases, our games may integrate the following services:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Serves advertisements. Depending on your consent and your device's settings, Google AdMob may process device identifiers (on iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA only with your permission, otherwise the identifier for vendors; on Android, the app set ID; our Android games do not request access to the Android advertising ID), coarse IP-based location, ad interaction data, and diagnostic signals for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config (Number Drop's measures): Where consent is required (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game sends nothing to Firebase until you agree in Google's consent form; elsewhere it starts once the game has checked with Google that no consent is required. Before then, and whenever consent is refused or withdrawn, events are dropped, never kept for later. The events say how the game is played: levels started and finished, with their result, the moves used and left, the stars and the jokers used; tutorial steps; running out of lives; rewarded ads offered and watched; visits to the shop and jokers bought with coins; and the daily challenge's streak. They hold numbers and the game's own short labels, never anything you type. Firebase adds its own events (first open, sessions, screens viewed), reads the stores' in-app purchases to count them, and, through its link to AdMob, reports what each ad earned. All of it is tied to a random ID Firebase makes for this installation, never to your name, an account or the advertising ID (the measures never read the iOS IDFA or the Android advertising ID), and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google derives an approximate location from your IP address, and receives your device's model, system and language. The game also downloads settings from Firebase Remote Config, to test changes such as the level before which the first ad between levels appears. Google processes this data for us, as our service provider.
  • Apple StoreKit 2 (In-App Purchases): All payment transactions are processed directly by Apple through the App Store. studio ailiers never receives or stores your payment details.
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases, Android): All payment transactions are processed directly by Google through Google Play. studio ailiers never receives or stores your payment details.
  • Apple Game Center (Leaderboards, Achievements & Challenges, iPhone): If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends Apple your scores (coins earned, stars won, your longest daily streak and today's daily challenge), the achievements you earn, and which daily challenge you play. Apple links them to your Game Center profile and shows them to the players your Game Center settings allow. You can play without signing in, in which case nothing is sent; you can sign out or change who sees you in iOS Settings → Game Center. See Game Center & Privacy.
  • Google Play Games (Leaderboards & Achievements, Android): If you are signed in to Play Games, the game sends Google your scores and the achievements you earn. Google links them to your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), shows them to the players your profile settings allow, and collects its own diagnostic data about the service. You can play without it; you can change your profile's visibility or delete your Play Games data in the Play Games app or at your Google Account.

studio ailiers runs no server of its own for these services: your scores and achievements go to Apple or Google only, and we never receive them.

To learn more about how Google processes data, please see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. GDPR / UK Consent (Google UMP), iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT / IDFA) & Android

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR / Google UMP): Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland are presented with a consent form via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are served or the game's measures are sent. You can review, change, or withdraw your consent choices at any time from PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's settings; refusing or withdrawing consent also stops the measures. Elsewhere, the measures are part of the game; uninstalling it stops them.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT / IDFA): On iOS, the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is never accessed unless you grant permission in Apple's system prompt. You can revoke or change this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: Our Android games do not access the Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete it at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads, depending on your device).

5. Legal Bases & Data Retention

Where GDPR / UK GDPR applies, data is processed based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising, tracking, and the game's measures (analytics and remote configuration), legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for security, fraud prevention, and crash diagnostics, and performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for in-app purchases and player support.

Local save data remains on your device until you reset progress or uninstall the app. Analytics event data in Google Firebase is retained for up to 14 months. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to withdraw consent at any time:

  • Update ad and measures consent in the game's PRIVACY CHOICES, in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data and app-scoped identifiers.
  • Manage or delete your Game Center data in iOS Settings → Game Center (and through privacy.apple.com), and your Play Games data in the Play Games app or your Google Account.
  • Contact us at contact@studioailiers.com.
