Privacy Policy

PLUSMINUS: Number Drop · Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Data Controller & Contact

PLUSMINUS: Number Drop is developed and published by studio ailiers (Louis Basseto & Matisse Sananes). The data controller responsible for processing personal data described in this policy is:

  • Controller: studio ailiers — Louis Basseto
  • Email: contact@studioailiers.com
  • Support & GitHub: Player Support · github.com/lbasseto

2. Offline & TestFlight Builds

In offline or pure TestFlight playtesting builds without advertising or analytics SDKs enabled, PLUSMINUS: Number Drop does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data off your device. Game progress, level completions, high scores, and preferences are saved strictly locally on your iPhone. For beta builds distributed via Apple TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and tester feedback in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Live & Commercial Builds (AdMob, Firebase & StoreKit 2)

In live and commercial App Store releases that include advertising, analytics, remote configuration, and in-app purchases, the game integrates the following services:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to serve banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements. Depending on your consent and iOS tracking permissions, Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the iOS Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA or app-set ID), coarse location derived from IP address, ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewards), and diagnostic signals for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Remote Configuration): Used to measure aggregate gameplay progression, level balance, app stability, and remote economy parameters. Firebase processes app-instance identifiers, coarse device information (device model, OS version), and in-game event telemetry.
  • Apple StoreKit 2 (In-App Purchases): All payment transactions and receipt verifications are processed directly by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit 2. studio ailiers never receives or stores your payment card or billing details.

To learn more about how Google processes data through AdMob and Firebase, please read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. GDPR / UK Consent (Google UMP) & iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT / IDFA)

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR / Google UMP): If you play from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game presents a consent form powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) built on the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) before serving ads. You can grant, customise, or refuse consent. You may review, change, or withdraw your consent choices at any time from the Privacy Settings option inside the game's settings menu.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT / IDFA): On iOS, the game never accesses the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) unless you explicitly grant permission in Apple's system App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can revoke or change this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline tracking, the game continues to work normally and serves only contextual (non-personalised) ads where applicable.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personal data is processed on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): For personalised advertising, cross-app tracking (IDFA), and non-essential analytics where consent is requested via UMP and ATT.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): For basic security, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics, and aggregated game-balance telemetry where permitted by law.
  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): For delivering purchased in-app items and responding to direct player support requests.

6. Data Retention

Local save data remains on your iPhone until you reset progress or uninstall the app. Analytics event data in Google Firebase is retained for up to 14 months before automatic expiration. Support emails are kept only for as long as needed to resolve your inquiry.

7. Your Privacy Rights (Access, Deletion & Opt-Out)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and California / US states), you have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of your personal data, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

  • Withdraw or update ad consent: Open the game's settings and select Privacy Settings, or toggle tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete local data: Uninstall the app from your iPhone to permanently remove all local save files and app-scoped identifiers.
  • Exercise rights by email: Contact us at contact@studioailiers.com and we will respond within the statutory timeframe.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game evolves. Any updates will be published at this URL (https://lbasseto.github.io/privacy.html) with a revised "Last updated" date.
